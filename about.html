My professional bio

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Green University of Bangladesh with a rare mix of commercial and technical experience. My career began on the business side: from January to June 2026 I generated leads and booked appointments for US and Canada-based clients as a Telesales Executive at BDR Outsourced Limited, verifying client data in CRM tools and producing daily and weekly performance reports. Since July 2026 I have been a Business Development Executive at Block B Solutions, handling procurement, market analysis, quotations and bids for government and non-government tenders, managing B2B client acquisition and RFQs, and building strong relationships with vendors.

I also worked as a Marketing Executive on the 32-day RapidPass project, where I helped promote the product to its audience. Together, these roles taught me how to understand customers, price competitively, negotiate with suppliers and communicate value clearly.

On the technical side, I am strong in SQL, Python and advanced Excel for reporting and decision making. My machine learning thesis built TabNet, XGBoost and Random Forest pipelines on structured datasets, and my university projects in C, Java, HTML, CSS and PHP include a Government Services Web Application and UI/UX prototypes. This background lets me read data, understand systems and work comfortably with technical teams.

Today I am looking to grow into a managerial role in operations management, sales or marketing, where I can combine process thinking, market insight and data-driven decisions to help a team perform better.
